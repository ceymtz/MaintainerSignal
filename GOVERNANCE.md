# Governance

MaintainerSignal currently follows a benevolent-maintainer model. The lead maintainer, @ceymtz, is responsible for releases, security response, roadmap decisions, and final review.

Substantial changes should begin with a public issue. Decisions prioritize maintainer safety, explainability, compatibility, privacy, and usefulness without AI. Recurring contributors may be invited as reviewers or maintainers based on sustained, constructive participation. Governance will evolve publicly as the contributor base grows.
