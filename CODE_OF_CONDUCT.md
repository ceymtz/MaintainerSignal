# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and protect private information. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
